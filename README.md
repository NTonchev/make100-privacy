<h1>Make 100 - Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="meta">Last updated: October 13, 2026</p>

<p>This policy explains what information the mobile game <strong>Make 100</strong> ("the game") uses and how. The game is published by <strong>NoisyTown Games</strong> ("we", "us"). Contact: <a href="mailto:noisytowngames@gmail.com">YOUR_EMAIL@example.com</a></p>

<h2>1. What we do not collect</h2>
<p>The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.</p>

<h2>2. Information stored on your device</h2>
<p>Your scores, streaks, settings, and tutorial progress are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless noted below.</p>

<h2>3. Leaderboard (Firebase)</h2>
<p>To show the global leaderboard, the game signs you in anonymously using Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your identity. We store this identifier together with your best streak and the time you reached it in Google Cloud Firestore. Other players only see a generated name such as "Player-1234" and your score.</p>
<p>Firebase may also process technical data such as your IP address and device or installation identifiers to provide its services. See the <a href="https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy">Firebase privacy information</a>.</p>

<h2>4. Advertising (Google AdMob)</h2>
<p>The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads can be personalized or non-personalized.</p>
<p>If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can accept, decline, or manage your choices. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. More information: <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services</a>.</p>

<h2>5. In-app purchases</h2>
<p>If the game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.</p>

<h2>6. Children</h2>
<p>The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>

<h2>7. Your rights</h2>
<p>Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because leaderboard records are anonymous, we may need extra details from you to find your record. Contact us at the email above and we will respond as soon as we can.</p>

<h2>8. Data retention</h2>
<p>Leaderboard records are kept while the game is available. Local data is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.</p>

<h2>9. Changes</h2>
<p>We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.</p>
