Make 100 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Make 100 ("the game") uses and how. The game is published by NoisyTown Games ("we", "us"). Contact: noisytowngames@gmail.com

1. What we do not collect

The game has no user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. Information stored on your device

Your scores, streaks, game settings, and tutorial progress are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is erased if you clear the app data or uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and technical details about your device and ad interactions. Depending on your region and choices, ads can be personalized or non-personalized.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game presents a consent banner (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads, allowing you to accept, decline, or manage your preferences. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, transactions are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your region (such as under the GDPR), you may have rights regarding your data, including the right to withdraw consent or contact your local data protection authority. Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, your local game progress is managed directly on your device. For any inquiries, contact us at the email above.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date at the top.
